# 🚀 Mission Survive

A browser-based astronaut survival game. Open `index.html` in any browser, no install needed.

Pick a destination (Moon, Mars, or Europa), manage oxygen, water, food, power, health and morale over a multi-day mission, and handle random events. Each event and action teaches a real fact about survival in space (radiation, ISRU, dust, comms delay, bone loss). Science points unlock real-world spin-off technologies, showing why space exploration matters on Earth.

## 🌑 Outpost 7

Open `outpost-7.html` in any browser. Six lunar outposts were lost within their first week. You command the seventh: **survive 30 days (one full lunar cycle) and bring home rock samples**.

- **NASA data:** the mission follows the real lunar day. About 29.5 Earth days per cycle: roughly 15 days of sunlight, then 15 days of night with no solar power. Surface temperature runs from about 127 °C in sunlight to about -173 °C at night. Gravity is 1.62 m/s². Rock samples carry real Apollo and LCROSS facts (Genesis Rock, Big Bertha, Apollo 17 orange soil, KREEP, ilmenite basalt, polar ice). Module and event descriptions cite real NASA tech and missions (Kilopower, the ISS water recycler and oxygen generator, MOXIE, Artemis rovers, Apollo 11's 1202 alarm, Apollo 17 dust, ISS suit leak, Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter).
- **Everything on one screen:** no sidebar. A HUD shows day, lunar phase, clock, temperature, energy, Science Points and all six survival bars. A hotbar opens floating panels (Survive, Science, Mission, Build, Base). Keys 1 to 5 open panels, V switches view, E uses things, B builds.
- **First-person mode:** press V (or the button) to walk the Moon through the astronaut's eyes: a raycast habitat interior, textured moon surface with parallax, craters, rocks, a horizon, the Sun and Earth, and a headlamp during the lunar night. Drag or use the arrow keys to look.
- **The Six Lost Outposts:** each failed crew has a named story (Pioneer, Dustfall, Sunward, Hullbreak, Frostline, Quiet Hall) with crew names, three log entries and the real NASA mission that taught the same lesson (Apollo 13, Opportunity, the 1972 solar storm, the 2018 Soyuz leak, lunar nights, ISS crew psychology). Find each wreck on the map and search it to read the logs. They collect in a new 📜 Archive panel (key 6). Reading all six gives a bonus, and the ending ends with a memorial.
- **Open world:** a large map you can explore anywhere, with a minimap. Walking outside costs time and suit oxygen. Find the wrecks of Outposts 1 to 6, eight rock sample sites, a shadowed ice crater and a lava tube cave.
- **20 buildable modules:** order kits with Science Points (Mission Control also sends +5 SP a day), pick them up at the landing pad, carry them anywhere and build. Power (solar, Kilopower reactor, fuel cell), food (greenhouse, hydroponics), water (extractor, recycler), air (scrubber, oxygen generator, regolith oxygen plant), shelter (radiation shelter, 3D printer), crew (rec room, medical annex, exercise module), science (observatory, seismometer, deep space antenna, sample lab) and a Lunar Terrain Vehicle garage that halves travel costs.
- **Win:** survive all 30 days, collect the required rock samples (4 to 6 by mode) and load them into the return capsule. A finish-mission sequence plays: dawn, liftoff, splashdown, lab results and a rank.
- Modes: Cadet, Junior, Commander, Veteran and Free Play (no failure).
- Land the outpost first (a soft landing keeps all supplies), plus a quiz and 46 junior astronaut science tasks for Science Points.
