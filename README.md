# 🚀 Mission Survive

A browser-based astronaut survival game. Open `index.html` in any browser, no install needed.

Pick a destination (Moon, Mars, or Europa), manage oxygen, water, food, power, health and morale over a multi-day mission, and handle random events. Each event and action teaches a real fact about survival in space (radiation, ISRU, dust, comms delay, bone loss). Science points unlock real-world spin-off technologies, showing why space exploration matters on Earth.

## 🌑 Outpost 7

Open `outpost-7.html` in any browser. Six lunar outposts were lost within their first week. You command the seventh: **survive 7 nights**.

- Land the outpost (soft landing keeps all supplies safe), then play 7 day and night cycles.
- Each day, spend limited energy on survival chores (oxygen, water, food, solar panel dust, exercise, morale, regolith shelter) and science tasks (46 junior astronaut activities that earn Science Points).
- Each night an event hits (dust storm, solar flare, micrometeorite, heater fault and more). You pick one of two options, each with real-world context and a debrief naming the real mission behind it.
- Spend Science Points on Mission Control supply drops, or take quizzes for more points.
- Modes: Cadet, Junior, Commander, Veteran and Free Play (no failure).
