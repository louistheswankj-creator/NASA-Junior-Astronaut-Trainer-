# 🚀 Mission Survive

A browser-based astronaut survival game. Open `index.html` in any browser, no install needed.

Pick a destination (Moon, Mars, or Europa), manage oxygen, water, food, power, health and morale over a multi-day mission, and handle random events. Each event and action teaches a real fact about survival in space (radiation, ISRU, dust, comms delay, bone loss). Science points unlock real-world spin-off technologies, showing why space exploration matters on Earth.

## 🌙 Space Sparks: Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

Open `space-sparks.html` in any browser. Take a lunar outpost from the first rover to a working base:

1. **Scout**: choose rover power, wheels, tools and dust protection (each option shows real-world context), then drive, collect the 4 Moon sample types and transmit them to Earth before the rover dies.
2. **Land**: fly the landing minigame on rover-scouted terrain until reliability reaches 75%, then send a crew in the Apollo-style Lunar Roving Vehicle.
3. **Build**: pick materials (aluminum, titanium, steel, honeycomb, carbon, Kapton, regolith covers), balance weight, strength and cost, and pass a stress test.
4. **Bonus**: Mars training with 1-sol command delay (queue 3 commands, the rover runs them blind).

Earn Science Points from missions, the Survival Lab (46 junior astronaut tasks) and quizzes. Points scale with complexity, price and value and fund new missions. Every mission ends with a debrief card naming the real mission behind the event (Opportunity, Curiosity, Pragyan, Perseverance, Apollo).
