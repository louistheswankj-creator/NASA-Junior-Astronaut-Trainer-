# 🚀 Mission Survive

A browser-based astronaut survival game. Open `index.html` in any browser, no install needed.

Pick a destination (Moon, Mars, or Europa), manage oxygen, water, food, power, health and morale over a multi-day mission, and handle random events. Each event and action teaches a real fact about survival in space (radiation, ISRU, dust, comms delay, bone loss). Science points unlock real-world spin-off technologies, showing why space exploration matters on Earth.

## 🌑 Outpost 7

Open `outpost-7.html` in any browser. Six lunar outposts were lost within their first week. You command the seventh: **survive 30 days (one full lunar cycle), stay under the radiation limit and bring home rock samples**. It is a choice-based survival game with no 2D or 3D world: every day you decide how to spend your hours, and every day brings problems.

- **Choices all day:** choose a landing site (Malapert Massif, Connecting Ridge or Haworth rim, three regions NASA studied for Artemis III, each with a different mix of dark days, ice, radiation and radio contact) and how to land. Then each day, spend limited hours on Maintain jobs, Science tasks, Explore trips, building modules or Mission Control quizzes.
- **Major problems happen often:** a morning problem (45% to 85% chance by mode) and a night event every day, each a choice with real trade-offs. 32 events across radiation, technical failures, medical emergencies, environment, supply delays and crew morale, plus EVA incidents on trips outside.
- **Radiation:** a real concern. About 1.4 mSv a day builds up on the surface, solar particle events and cosmic ray surges add more, and every trip outside adds dose. Shelters, regolith burial and the Haworth crater walls cut it. Radiation sickness starts at 120 mSv and 250 mSv ends the mission. NASA's career limit is 600 mSv.
- **Actual NASA data:** the lunar day (about 29.5 Earth days), temperatures (about 127 °C to -173 °C and below -246 °C in shadowed craters), 1.62 m/s² gravity, 1.3 second radio delay, oxygen needs, ISS water recycling, Apollo and LCROSS sample facts, and a different real NASA fact each day. Events and modules cite real missions (Apollo 11's 1202 alarm, Apollo 13's CO2 filter, the 2013 ISS suit leak, Mir's fire, the 1972 solar storm, Kilopower, MOXIE, Artemis).
- **Build stuff:** order any of 20 modules or supply drops with Science Points, wait 2 days for the cargo lander, spend hours to build, and choose to place it on the surface or bury it under regolith for extra shielding.
- **Explore:** trips to 8 rock sample sites, the shadowed ice crater, a lava tube and the wrecks of Outposts 1 to 6, costing hours, oxygen and dose.
- **The Six Lost Outposts:** each failed crew has a named story with crew, logs and a real NASA lesson. Find the wrecks to read them in the Archive.
- **Win:** survive all 30 days, collect the required rock samples (4 to 6 by mode) and load them into the return capsule. A finish-mission sequence plays.
- Modes: Cadet, Junior, Commander, Veteran and Free Play (no failure).
